COMMON QUESTIONS

Before you enquire.

Quick answers about D3 workshop services and how to request a quotation.

What services does D3 provide?

We provide CNC cutting and carving, panel cutting, edge banding, mica pressing, multi-boring and furniture component job work.

Can carpenters and furniture makers send job-work requirements?

Yes. Carpenters, furniture makers, designers and contractors can contact us with drawings, measurements, material details and quantities.

How can I get a quotation?

Call or WhatsApp us with your drawing, reference image, dimensions, material and required quantity. We will discuss the work and quotation.

Where is D3 Interior Designs & Production located?

Our company location is in the Attingal, Korani and Thettichira area of Kerala. Use the Google Maps link on this page for directions.
